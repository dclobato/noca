Sign-in lockouts

After repeated failures the UberAdmin login, the contest login, the password re-confirmation and the animator control gate refuse a login or an address for a while. Lift that early here. Every unlock is password-confirmed and recorded in the security-event log. Unlocking something that is not locked changes nothing, so it is safe to try.

Unlock an address
